Privacy Policy

This application accesses Google Drive solely to authorise and manage the developer's own Google account for personal backup and self-hosted project purposes. No data is collected, logged, stored, or transmitted to any third party or server operated by this site.

The OAuth tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Any Client ID, Client Secret, authorisation code, or token you enter or receive is sent directly from your browser to Google's own servers and is never transmitted to, or stored by, this website or its operator.

This tool is intended for use solely by its developer, with their own Google Cloud project credentials, and is not intended for use by the general public.
